## Tool
I will develop a browser-based Stroop colour-identification experiment using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The game will record the trial condition ((congruent or incongruent))., response, accuracy, and reaction time in milliseconds. I will compare the average reaction times for correct responses across the two conditions and examine response accuracy. The results will be summarized using a graph (( a bar graph)) and a CSV file containing trial-level data.
